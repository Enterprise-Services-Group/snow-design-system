Faculty of Science Lab Reuse Initiative

Every year the Faculty disposes of working equipment, glassware and unopened consumables simply because one lab no longer needs them. The Lab Reuse Initiative connects those items with researchers and teaching staff who do — saving money, cutting waste and keeping usable kit out of landfill.

What is the Lab Reuse Initiative?

A Faculty-wide program, supported by the Sustainability Office, that lists surplus laboratory items in a single online store. Staff and students across Western Edge can browse what is available, request items free of charge, and arrange collection with help from the reuse team.

Free to donate

Listing an item takes about ten minutes. The Sustainability Office covers approved transport costs, so clearing your lab does not come out of your grant.

Free to receive

Items in the store are offered at no cost to Faculty of Science labs, teaching program and student projects. You only need to say how the item will be used.

Safety checked

Every listing records condition, hazardous material history, decontamination status and handling requirements before it goes live.

Share, don't only give

Not ready to part with something? List it for occasional access or open shared use and keep it in your lab while others book time on it.

How the process works

Five steps from the moment you decide an item is surplus to the moment it lands in its new lab.

1

List the item

Complete the donation form with photos, condition, dimensions and any safety history. You will need your supervisor's name for approval.

2

Review and approval

The reuse team checks the submission, confirms safety details and assigns a unique item ID such as ES‑014.

3

Published to the store

The listing appears in the online store where researchers can filter by category, condition, availability and access requirements. Some items may also be transported to the reuse store on campus to view in person.

4

Request and match

An interested researcher adds the item to their cart and submits a request explaining the intended use. The reuse team confirms the match with you.

5

Collection and handover

Collection is scheduled, usually within five to fifteen business days. Campus assist or an external contractor moves the item and the listing is closed.

6

Final call date

If no one requests an item by its final call date, the reuse team contacts you to agree on external donation, recycling or disposal.

Common questions

Does it cost anything?

No. Listing and receiving items is free. Approved transport within Western Edge is covered by the Sustainability Office. Specialist disconnection or external contractor work is discussed case by case.

What can I donate?

Small and large equipment, specialised instruments, glassware and plasticware, unopened consumables, storage and lab furniture. Items may be fully working, working with minor issues, or listed for parts — just describe the condition honestly.

What about items that held hazardous material?

They can still be listed. You must record the hazardous material history and the decontamination steps taken. The reuse team will not publish a listing until that information is complete.

Can I keep using the item?

Yes. Choose "occasional access" if you need it back for short periods, or "open shared donation" if the item stays in your lab and others book time on it.

How long does it take?

Approval usually takes a few business days. Collection is typically scheduled within five to fifteen business days once a match is confirmed.
