Science Equipment Reuse Initiative

Use this form to donate or offer surplus laboratory equipment, consumables, glassware, lab furniture or storage shelving for reuse across the University. This pilot supports donation and swap only. Sales, chemicals and hazardous waste are out of scope.

CONDITION

  • Excellent - Near-new condition, no visible wear or damage, complete and ready to reuse.
  • Good - Minor cosmetic wear only, complete and suitable for reuse.
  • Fair - Noticeable wear or minor missing accessories, but still usable with caveats.
  • Poor - Significant wear, damage or incomplete parts; may require repair or assessment before reuse.
  • Fully working - Performs as expected and can be reused without repair.
  • Working with issues - Usable, but has known limitations, faults or intermittent problems.
  • Needs repair - Not ready for use until repaired, serviced or assessed.
  • Not working - Does not currently function.
  • Unknown - Functional status has not been confirmed.
