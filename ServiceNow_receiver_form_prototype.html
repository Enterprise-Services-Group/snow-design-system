    ITEM VIEWINGS

    Select any items you would like to view before confirming the request. The reuse store team will contact the donor to arrange viewing where possible. Viewing availability depends on the donor and on whether the item is at the reuse store or still in the donor's lab.
    i
    If an item is listed as being in the reuse store, you are welcome to come in during store hours to view it — no booking is needed. Items still in a donor's lab require the reuse store team to arrange access with the donor.

    To minimise delays, viewings are not normally offered for consumables or standard glassware and plasticware. Requests to view specialised glassware or other unique items will be considered case-by-case by the Reuse Store team.
